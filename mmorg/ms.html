Monka Stick


Monka Stick is a MMORG game where you give the stick a command and it does something. A command list is below.
  • spin: makes the monka stick spin around 10 times.
  • colorchanger: like the spin command, but it turns magenta.
  • Kristen: makes it turn red.
  • apple: makes a green circle appear. (click "hide apple" to hide it.)
  • snake: makes a yellow snake image pop up. (click "hide snake" to hide it.)
  • heart: makes a red heart pop up. (click "hide heart" to hide it).
  • tardi: makes a tardigrade pop up. (click "hide tardigrade" to hide it.)

Lucky

Lucky is a MMORG game where one random yellow square is chosen to be the lucky one. If you click on it, you get a point.
If you have enough PX, you can buy stuff.
PX Deal name Effect
5 Doubler Adds 2px if you get the lucky square.
10 Item 2 Adds 0.5px if you aren't lucky
20 Quadrupler Adds 4px if you get the lucky square

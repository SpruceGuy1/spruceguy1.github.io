# Game start

Your player is in the top left corner. Use WASD, arrow keys, or buttons at the bottom to move your player. Click on trees to harvest them. Spruce and birch trees give wood, apple trees give wood or apples, and maple trees give wood or maple syrup. You can view your inventory from the dropdown saying "Inventory".

# Crafting

Using wood and other resources, you can craft things. Select an item from the dropdown menu and then click "Craft" to craft.

# Fighting

Any mob (sherp, cow, chicken, pig, zombie) can be knocked by one block by clicking on them.
